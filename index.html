WordPress / WooCommerce diagnostic help

Your site is broken, slow, or losing orders. Let’s find the real cause before you pay for the wrong fix.

A focused diagnostic sprint for small businesses that need a clear, practical repair plan for WordPress, WooCommerce, plugins, APIs, forms, performance, or checkout issues.

Ask for a diagnostic review No blind promises. No “fixed in one hour” talk. First we check the problem, then we tell you the safest next step.

When this helps

  • WooCommerce orders are not reaching your CRM, API, warehouse, or payment workflow.
  • A plugin, theme, or WordPress update broke layout, forms, checkout, or admin behavior.
  • Your site feels slow and you do not know whether the issue is hosting, code, plugins, images, or tracking scripts.
  • You received mixed advice from developers and want a clear technical second opinion.

What you get

  • A short list of likely causes and visible risks.
  • A practical priority order: what to fix first and what can wait.
  • A next-step plan you can give to a developer or use for a follow-up project.
  • An honest answer on whether we can help further.

How the sprint works

1

You share the site URL, symptoms, screenshots, error messages, and what changed before the problem appeared.

2

We review the problem from the outside first, then ask only for the access or details needed for a safe diagnosis.

3

You receive a clear repair path: likely cause, risk level, first fix to try, and what should not be touched yet.

Why not jump straight into “fixing”?

Because many WordPress problems look simple from the outside and become expensive when someone changes production without checking dependencies, backups, plugin conflicts, or order flow.

What we do not promise blindly

We do not guarantee revenue, rankings, exact delivery dates, or a fixed price before looking at the system. That keeps the work safer and more honest for both sides.
